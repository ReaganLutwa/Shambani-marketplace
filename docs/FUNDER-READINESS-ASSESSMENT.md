# ShambaNi pre-pilot readiness assessment

**Assessment date:** 9 September 2026
**Decision:** Not ready for a funding or government pilot request. Ready for a limited technical-review request after the evidence gaps below are closed.

## Verified implementation state

ShambaNi is a public React/Vite prototype hosted as a static site. It demonstrates multilingual marketplace screens, sample listings, a proposed USSD menu, paused registration journeys and a sample operations view. It does not include a production backend, database, authentication, identity verification, order processing, payment integration, escrow, telecom connection or allocated USSD code.

The public prototype has been corrected to state this status clearly. Sensitive registration fields and simulated checkout completion have been disabled.

## Readiness scorecard

| Area | Current evidence | Gate before pilot | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product concept | Navigable public prototype | Structured user testing with findings | Partial |
| Farmer and buyer demand | No signed evidence in reviewed pack | Interview log and signed letters of interest | Missing |
| Backend and data | No production service | Authenticated API, database, backups and audit logging | Missing |
| Data protection | Privacy notice added; no certificate evidenced | PDPO registration and documented lawful processing | Missing |

| Pilot operations | Proposed workflow and targets | Named pilot lead, incident route, participant consent and signed district/site permission | Partial |
| Evidence and monitoring | Sample dashboard | Baseline, metric definitions, source records and reporting owner | Missing |

## Recommended decision request

Request a **technical review meeting**, not funding, endorsement, data access or national rollout. Demonstrate the corrected prototype and ask the responsible Uganda institution to confirm the proper pilot pathway, applicable standards and focal point. Do not use government logos or imply partnership without written permission.

## Minimum controlled-pilot proof

Use Mpigi and Wakiso as proposed locations only after local permission. A credible first validation target is 20 farmers, 5 buyers and 10 manually evidenced completed orders over 90 days. Treat these as targets, not achievements. Record consent, order terms, delivery acceptance, incidents and feedback. Publish no personal data.

## Go/no-go rule

No farmer identity data, payment, USSD launch or government submission should proceed until every applicable item in the government checklist is marked complete and linked to dated evidence.
