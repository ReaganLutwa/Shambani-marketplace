# Uganda Government engagement readiness checklist

**Prepared:** 9 September 2026  
**Current recommendation:** Do not submit a funding or rollout request yet. First request a technical-review meeting after the blocking items are evidenced.

## Important timing

The 2026 Uganda Government Systems Prototype Showcase listed agriculture/agribusiness as eligible, but its published deadline was **1 June 2026**. That round is closed as of this checklist date. Do not represent ShambaNi as an applicant or selected system unless the Government Digital Repository confirms another open route in writing.

## Eligibility and evidence

| Item | Current status | Required action/evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Working prototype | Complete for demonstration | Keep the public pre-pilot labels and provide the repository/deployment link |
| System description, maximum five pages | Being rebuilt | Use the corrected ShambaNi pre-pilot brief |
| Architecture and screenshots | Partial | Attach the USSD architecture and current labelled screenshots |
| Founder identification or URSB registration | Not evidenced in reviewed files | Founder supplies a valid National ID copy or current URSB documents as the call permits |
| URA tax clearance | Not evidenced in reviewed files | Founder obtains and supplies the current certificate if required by the target call |
| PDPO registration | Not evidenced | Complete before collecting or processing personal data where registration is required |
| USSD approval/provider contract | Missing | Obtain written UCC/operator/aggregator route and technical confirmation |
| Payment provider agreement | Missing | Select and contract with a Bank of Uganda-licensed provider before accepting payments |
| Farmer demand evidence | Missing | Conduct and document at least 10 structured interviews; obtain consented letters of interest |
| Buyer demand evidence | Missing | Obtain at least 3–5 dated institutional letters of interest with no confidential procurement data |
| Pilot location permission | Missing | Obtain written permission from proposed Mpigi/Wakiso sites or relevant local counterparts |
| Security evidence | Missing | Complete threat model, access design, backup/restore test, incident plan and independent review |
| Budget and funding source | Missing | Prepare a costed 90-day pilot budget with assumptions and contingencies |
| Team capacity | Partial | Name accountable product, technical, operations, privacy and finance owners |

“Not evidenced” means the document was not present in the reviewed project pack; it is not a claim that the founder does not possess it.

## Safe first request to Government

Ask for:

- a 30-minute technical review of the prototype and proposed pilot controls;
- confirmation of the correct institutional focal point and submission route;
- guidance on interoperability, data-protection and pilot documentation expectations;
- permission to return with a 90-day controlled-pilot proposal after the gates are complete.

Do not initially ask for endorsement, access to government systems or citizen data, use of a government logo, national deployment, procurement preference or public funding.

## 90-day validation proposal

Subject to approval and resources:

| Measure | Target | Evidence source |
|---|---:|---|
| Consented farmers completing onboarding test | 20 | Dated consent/onboarding register |
| Institutional or commercial buyers testing workflow | 5 | Signed participation/interest record |
| Completed, manually verified produce orders | 10 | Order, delivery and acceptance records |
| Critical privacy/security incidents | 0 | Incident register |
| Farmer sessions completed without staff takeover | Baseline then improve | Observed task-test log |

These are proposed targets, not current achievements.

## Go/no-go gates

Do not collect identity documents until the privacy and secure-storage gates are complete. Do not accept money until a licensed provider, reconciliation process and dispute route are active. Do not launch USSD until the code/provider and end-to-end acceptance gates are evidenced. Stop the pilot if consent, participant safety, funds or personal data cannot be protected.
